## UNFILED 54: H3, fix/pint-on-the-left-of-a-uw-quantity at 08a3d944 (on development fbef72fa). Verdict: READY

**Method.** The branch is Python-only (`function/quantities.py`, plus a test). It was built at 08a3d944. Development is the same build with fbef72fa's `quantities.py` in place.

**(a)** `test_0781`: 5 failed on development, 5 passed here.

**(b) pint-left against uw-left against pure pint**, on the branch:
- **`*`, `/`, `+`, `-`:** all three agree, including mixed km and m and a pint array on the left. On development pint-left gave wrong units (6 m / 2 m = 3 m; 6 m / 2 s = 3 m) or raised DimensionalityError for + and -.
- **Offset units:**
  - degC - degC: pint-left 10 delta_degC, uw-left 10 K. The same quantity, labelled differently.
  - degC + 5 delta_degC: 298.15 K on both sides; pure pint gives 25 degC, the same.
  - degC + degC: raises on both sides, as pure pint does.
  - K - degC: 6.85 K on both sides.

**(c) Ra in magnitude, not just in format.** The hand value is ρgαΔTL³/(ηκ) = 1.2625e6 (ΔT 1300 K). The example's κ is in m²/s, where dropping its units leaves the number unchanged, so the same Ra was recomputed with κ in other units:

| κ given as | development | 08a3d944 |
|---|---|---|
| 1e-6 m²/s | 1.2625e6 [m²/s] | 1.2625e6, dimensionless |
| 1 mm²/s | **1.2625** [m²/s] | 1.2625e6, dimensionless |
| 3.6e-3 m²/hour | **350.7** [m²/s] | 1.2625e6, dimensionless |

**(d)** np 2: `test_0781` 5 passed on both ranks; the Ra probe is identical on both ranks.

**Findings.** Both are pre-existing on development, outside this branch, and suitable for a new UNFILED row.
1. Low: an offset unit times a number is asymmetric. `uw.quantity(20, "degC") * uw.quantity(2, "dimensionless")` silently gives 586.3 K (degC to K, then the product). pint refuses it (OffsetUnitCalculusError), and pint-left now refuses it too.
2. Low: comparisons between pint and `uw.quantity` are wrong in both orders. `(6 m) == uw.quantity(6, "m")` and the reverse are False, and `<` raises ValueError. Pure pint gives True and False.
