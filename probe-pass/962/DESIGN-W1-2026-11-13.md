# #962: design page (W1, 2026-11-13)

*Copied by the dev hub from W1's message; W1's file is on its own machine only. By reading
(PETSc 3.25.0 source, 3.25.3 spot-checked, development a7297194); nothing run. Uses H1's region
numbers: `extract_region("Inner")` at np 4 gives cells [27, 0, 41, 0]; ByKey assemblies [2, 1, 2, 1].*

**The lever.** Only one branch of `DMPlexComputeJacobianByKey` is at fault: two kernel sets AND two
different matrices. When the caller passes the same matrix twice PETSc itself sets
`hasPrec = PETSC_FALSE` (line 6255 in 3.25.3) and every rank assembles once. That is why Poisson works.

**Candidate 1 (upstream): do it; fixes nothing for users for months.** Every environment we ship takes
conda-forge's binary (the runtime environment holds `petsc-3.25.3-real_h93af1a4_0`); only the two `amr`
environments build PETSc. We cannot carry a patch without building PETSc.

**Candidate 2, recommended: our own local Jacobian callback, two calls.**
- `DMPlexSNESComputeJacobianFEM(dm, X, J, J)`: Jacobian kernels only, into J.
- `DMPlexSNESComputeJacobianFEM(dmP, X, P, P)`: `dmP` is a clone whose system carries the preconditioner
  kernels as its Jacobian, and no boundary kernels.
- Cannot invert: neither call has two kernel sets with two matrices, on fixed or unfixed PETSc. No
  collective added, none rank-conditional, no version test.
- The solver's own system is untouched: SNES still makes two matrices and the Schur pmat is the same matrix.
- Expected bitwise identical J and P (same kernel pointers, quadrature, insertion order). Boundary terms
  too: today a boundary Jacobian goes into J only in Stokes (no boundary preconditioner is registered;
  `DMPlexComputeBdJacobianSingleByLabel` then inserts into `Jac`), and it still would. To be shown by
  `MatEqual` on J and P over the Stokes tests, serial and np 2 to 8.
- Cost: one more sweep of geometry and field interpolation per Jacobian; kernels are not evaluated more
  often (uu, up, pu are already integrated twice today). Not measured.
- Size: C callback about 25 lines; the second system where the kernels are registered (Stokes
  11902-11908, multiplier 11973), sharing sections, auxiliary vector and constants, 100 to 150 lines; tests.
- One path or two is Louis's call with the register row: always (every Stokes test exercises it; needs the
  bitwise table and the cost), or only when the minimum over ranks of local cells is 0 (an exact integer,
  decided once at setup by all ranks). W1 would take "always" if the matrices are bitwise equal and the
  cost is small.
- Risks: the second system must follow every rebuild of the first; the callback must find `dmP` from the
  DM it is handed (composed on it), since DMSNES state is copied to other DMs (`copyDS(coarse_dm)` has 11 sites).

**Struck.** A collective added on empty ranks (inverts when PETSc is fixed). One matrix for J and P with a
separate Schur pmat (changes the fieldsplit set-up).

**Candidate 3 (sub-communicator): right for cost, not needed for correctness, large; roadmap.**
`DMPlexFilter` builds on the parent's communicator; 530 uses of `uw.mpi.comm` / `COMM_WORLD` /
`uw.mpi.size` / `uw.mpi.rank` in 57 files; a user script runs on every rank.

**Struck: redistribute the region.** `_preserve_partition=True` keeps a region's points on the rank
holding the parent's, and `restrict`, `prolongate`, the vertex map and the coordinate sync are rank-local
(`_build_dof_map` is a kd-tree match of DOF coordinates at 1e-10). Redistribution needs a parallel
transfer we do not have, and still leaves empty ranks when the region has fewer cells than ranks.

**Same shape elsewhere:** `DMPlexComputeJacobianByKeyGeneral` (same defect; not on our path).
`DMPlexComputeResidualByKey`, the boundary residual and Jacobian, `DMPlexComputeJacobianHybridByKey`:
leave early but skip no collective. `DMPlexComputeJacobian_Patch_Internal`: no early exit.

**Not seen yet:** a Stokes solve getting past the first Jacobian with an empty rank (fieldsplit, sub-DMs,
null space, pressure mean, rotated walls, multigrid on such a mesh are unmeasured).

**Order W1 proposes:** (1) the refusal, row 16; (2) candidate 2 on a branch, bitwise table serial, then
H1 runs the region case and test_1140 at np 2 to 8; the refusal goes in the same PR if the solve
completes; (3) upstream draft. W1 does not start (2) until Louis has seen this.

**Side finding (for #1072):** no boundary preconditioner is registered, so a Stokes solve's P has no
Nitsche or natural-BC Jacobian terms at all; they are in J only. Possibly why the Nitsche Stokes in
test_1060 diverges. W1 measures it when it reaches #1072.
