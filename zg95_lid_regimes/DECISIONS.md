# Decisions ledger: the zg95 fault and rollback drivers

These drivers (`drivers/annulus_faults.py`, `annulus_fault.py`, `box_fault.py`, `annulus_model.py`)
are campaign code in `uw3-campaigns`, not part of underworld3. Every choice they make that the
library does not make for them is a campaign decision and is recorded here, dated, with the
evidence and who ruled. The hub's day-to-day record is `HUB.md` at the repository root; the
pages linked there hold the figures. A run's own `README.md` carries a "Choices" block
(every knob below, its value, set or default) and `run_info.json` the resolved parameters,
since 094e4f3. Louis, 2026-11-01: "every exposed knob is a recorded choice"; 2026-11-02:
these decisions must be findable by other Claudes and by people, not only by the hub.

Newest first within each section. "Ruled" means Louis decided; "measured" means the value
rests on a kaiju measurement cited; "taken" means a default inherited without a decision.

## Fault placement and geometry

| knob | value | status | evidence / ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **the fault's discretisation (principle)** | the ladder's rungs are the fault's own points; the band width is the local mesh size along the fault (an average where the mesh is not uniform); neither is a knob | ruled 2026-11-03 (Louis, clarifying decision 1b): "the along-fault resolution is set by the discretisation of the fault, the fault width is set by the local mesh size (which may not be uniform everywhere that the fault is embedded, but an average of some sort seems sensible here). What is going wrong in our model is that we are also defining the fault and its discretisation as a knob we can control. Generally this is not true - the ladder is built from the fault points and we can see if that is sufficient or not, but we generally can't just change it." | consequence for the drivers (design with kaiju, 2026-11-03): `uw_spacing` goes; the locator's points are the rungs and are produced at the local cell size, never resampled to a chosen S; W = the local cell size (h_b on the uniform annulus, i.e. one cell wide, against 2S = 2 h_b in recipe v2); the record states the division actually used (rung count, rung length mean and range, W) so sufficiency can be seen, not set; the restart rule re-divides a restored polyline only when the mesh differs. Recipe v2's numbers on the uniform annulus match the principle in rung spacing but not in width. |
| `uw_fault_fit` (crust-base locator) | envelope (the driver default since kaiju/envelope-default; parabola kept as the old behaviour) | ruled by the go on the Earth plan, 2026-10-29 | 40/40 stored checkpoints fit, monotone, 0.0055 trace change under 10 % particle dropout; the parabola fit 29/40 and moved 0.75. | `uw_wall_normal` | analytic (unit_r) | Louis ruled geometric 2026-11-02; **held on a known mechanism**: with a placed fault the geometric normal admits an arbitrary rigid rotation of the whole annulus (kaiju jobs 3447/3454: omega -1543 at inset 1, -4743 at inset 3; the flow is identical to the analytic answer once omega x r is removed; slip unchanged).
