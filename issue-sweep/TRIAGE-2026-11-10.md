# underworld3 open-issue triage (2026-11-10)

Read-only triage of the 254 open issues of `underworldcode/underworld3`. The focus is the
**174 issues not labelled `fixed-in-development`**: 111 unlabelled and 63 labelled. The 80
`fixed-in-development` issues are left aside except for the label check in section 3.4.

Evidence used: full issue bodies and comments (fetched 2026-11-10), the bodies of 491 merged
PRs into `development` and of 15 open PRs, and code on `origin/development` at `a6eebb61`
(2026-11-10). "Verified" means I read the code on `development`. It does not mean I ran anything.
Nothing was posted, labelled or edited on GitHub.

A caution on the matching: this repository's PR bodies almost never say "Fixes #N" for an
issue that is still open. They list issues as **"Left open"** or **"Still live"**. Most of the
PR-to-issue cross-references I found are of that kind, so I did not count a mention as a fix.
The fixes I report below come from three kinds of evidence: the lead's own "fixed on
development" comment, a PR that names the issue's exact mechanism, or code on `development`
that carries the fix and a test that names the issue.

**Where one owner would retire the most issues:**

1. **Units: the dimensional/non-dimensional boundary.** C6, plus #763 and #765 from C13: 17 issues. Two root causes cover most of it. The first is `evaluate`'s contract: it takes non-dimensional input and returns SI output (#771 is the root, #786, #843, #881 and #904 follow from it). The second is physical versus model coordinates in mesh moves (#979, #989, #991, #998). Open PR #1003 already fixes #989 and part of #979.
2. **The mesh-move PR stack, then one storage-layout helper.** C8, C9 and #682: about 14 issues. Landing #959, #982, #988, #1003 and #1024 fixes or partly fixes #531, #960, #966, #979, #984, #989, #994, #1000 and #1005. A single layout helper (#1006) would then retire most of #984, #995, #1000, #1004 and #1005.
3. **Rotated free slip.** C1: 14 issues, in three root-cause groups. The first is corners where a rotated wall meets a pinned component (#608, #939, #940, #941). The second is rigid-rotation admission (#968, #969). The third is convergence-reference timing (#883, #884, and #1011 in C5).
4. **Custom MG and FAC hierarchy.** C2 groups (a) and (b), plus #1020: about 10 issues. This is the hierarchy lifecycle after the mesh changes (#898, #1017, #1019, #1020) and empty-rank hygiene in fault-split MG (#873, #874, #885, #888). #897 is an API gap on the same object.
5. **Adjoint re-verification.** C13: 12 issues. Seven carry a stale comment saying the adjoint is not on development, but #744 merged on 2026-10-13. One probe session against development could close or confirm all of them. #766 and #804 look fixed; #765 and #781 look live.
6. **ThinSheet and manifold meshes.** C4: 10 issues, all filed from the review of #889. Each has a TODO(BUG) marker.
7. **Solver caps and convergence policy.** C5: #585, #625, #755, #770, #779, #1011. One decision on the sub-solve iteration caps covers #585, #625 and #755. #770 and #779 look superseded by #1014.
8. **Transport and stress history.** C11: 15 issues, but the root causes differ. Landing open PR #800 first fixes #682 and touches #797 and #799.
