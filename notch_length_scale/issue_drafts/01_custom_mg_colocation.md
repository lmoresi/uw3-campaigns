Title: custom_mg: set_custom_fmg leaves independent coarse meshes uncolocated in parallel; orphaned coarse DOFs make the preconditioner fail (NaN) at np >= 12

## Summary

`set_custom_fmg(solver, coarse_meshes, ...)` leaves **independent (non-nested) coarse meshes partitioned on their own** in parallel. A fine node and the coarse cell over it then sit on different ranks. The cross-partition transfer misses a large share of coarse DOFs. Nearest-fine-DOF injection "repairs" them with only a warning, and the preconditioner then fails: a NaN norm, or `DIVERGED_PC_FAILED` at iteration 0.

`adopt_hierarchy` already avoids this by co-locating every coarse level with the finest (`_colocate_level`). Its docstring records the same failure on the S-fault rig ("488 of 5614 at np=2, and the repaired transfer does not precondition"). The solver-level `set_custom_fmg` route skips that step.

## Observed

The case is a 3-D Stokes problem (micromorphic notch, P2/P1, coupled P1 field in the velocity split). Its hierarchy is two independent gmsh meshes (425 and 1052 tets) under a 4447-tet solver mesh, with no nested refinement (ref 0). Orphan coarse DOFs reported by the `custom_mg` warnings:

| ranks | transfer 0→1 | transfer 1→2 | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 119 / 6 | 7 | converges |
| 12 | 918 | 2221 | `KSPSolve has not converged due to infinity or NaN norm` |
| 12, coarse levels co-located with `_colocate_level` | 1065 | — (clean) | fails |
| 16, only the 1052-tet level, co-located | — | — (clean) | converges, same Newton history as nested-only |

So co-locating fixes the transfer onto the solver mesh. But the transfer **between two independent coarse levels** (425 → 1052 tets) still orphans about half of the coarse DOFs at np 12–16, even co-located (np 4: 119). I have not explained that part.

## Suggested fix

1. In `set_custom_fmg` (and `CustomMGHierarchy` when it is handed meshes), co-locate every coarse level that is not a native `refine()` slot with the finest level in parallel, as `adopt_hierarchy` does. Leave native refine levels alone: they are already co-partitioned, and moving them breaks the exact nested transfer.
2. Make the orphan repair fail loudly beyond a small threshold (say >1% of a level's coarse DOFs). A transfer that silently injects into hundreds of DOFs does not precondition, and the failure then surfaces far away, as a NaN in KSPSolve.
3. Find why the coarse-to-coarse transfer orphans so many DOFs at high rank counts after co-location. Co-locating each coarse level against the finest does not co-locate the two coarse levels with each other. Co-locating each level against its next-finer level may be the right rule.

Workaround used: co-locate in the driver, and use a single gmsh coarse level.

Found on `feature/shear-band-length-scale` (custom_mg as on development, plus a multi-field transfer that does not touch the parallel builders).
